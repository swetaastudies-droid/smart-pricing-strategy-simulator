# 🚀 Smart Pricing Strategy Simulator for Startups

An interactive **Python and Streamlit-based pricing strategy simulator** designed to help startups evaluate different pricing scenarios and understand their potential impact on customer demand, revenue, profitability, customer acquisition, and unit economics.

The project uses **customer segmentation, pricing simulation, and machine learning techniques** to support data-driven pricing decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Pricing is one of the most important strategic decisions for a startup. A price that is too low may reduce profitability, while a price that is too high may affect customer demand and acquisition.

The **Smart Pricing Strategy Simulator for Startups** provides a simulated environment where different pricing strategies can be tested before making real-world pricing decisions.

For demonstration purposes, the project uses a hypothetical SaaS startup:

### 💼 BizPilot AI

**BizPilot AI** is a hypothetical AI-powered SaaS platform designed to provide business analytics and insights for small businesses and startups.

The simulator evaluates multiple subscription price points and analyzes their potential impact on:

- Customer demand
- Customer retention
- Revenue
- Variable costs
- Contribution margin
- Profitability
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- LTV:CAC ratio
- CAC payback period

> **Important:** BizPilot AI is a hypothetical startup used for simulation purposes. The customer data, costs, demand assumptions, and financial results in this project are synthetic assumptions and should not be interpreted as actual business data.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- To simulate different startup pricing strategies
- To understand customer willingness-to-pay patterns
- To segment customers based on willingness to pay and churn probability
- To evaluate revenue and profitability across multiple price points
- To incorporate customer churn into pricing analysis
- To calculate customer acquisition economics
- To estimate Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- To calculate LTV:CAC and CAC payback
- To apply machine learning for pricing recommendation support
- To provide an interactive Streamlit dashboard
- To compare simulated pricing scenarios with external SaaS pricing references
